## 5. 特别条款与限制

**Exclusions / 免责条款**:

1. Intentional acts or gross negligence by the insured / 被保险人故意行为或重大过失
2. Driving under the influence of alcohol or drugs / 酒后或药物影响下驾驶
3. Driving without valid license / 无有效驾驶证驾驶
4. Using vehicle for illegal commercial purposes / 非法商用
5. Racing or speed competitions / 赛车或速度竞赛
6. War, terrorism, or nuclear risks / 战争、恐怖主义或核风险

**Conditions / 条件**:

1. Driver must hold valid driving license during accident / 驾驶员事故时必须持有有效驾驶证
2. Vehicle must be used for personal use only / 车辆必须仅用于个人使用
3. No illegal commercial or taxi operations / 不得非法营运或出租车经营
4. Immediate notification required after accident / 事故后须立即通知保险公司
5. Police report required for third party claims / 涉及第三方索赔需提供警方报告
